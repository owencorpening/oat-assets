GENERATION PROMPTS
------------------

Prompt (cornerstone.png — perspective view):
i need a graphic: as though it were etched into the corner of a
granite building, the text "O A T"

Prompt (frontCornerstone.png — brass plaque straight-on):
Sequence of three prompts applied in order:

1. Photorealistic polished gray granite block, straight-on view,
flat face filling the frame. No perspective distortion.
Raking light from upper left casting shadows into carved cuts.
Three lines of deeply incised V-cut lettering centered on the face:
Line 1: Large Roman capital letters O A T — widely spaced,
approximately 60% of block height. The A has an engineering
stencil quality — clean apex, no serifs on the A only.
O and T are classical Roman.
Thin horizontal ruled line incised into stone below the letters.
Line 2: OWEN'S APPLIED THINKING — spaced serif capitals,
much smaller than OAT, centered, letter-spaced wide.
Thin horizontal ruled line.
Line 3: EST. MMXXV — centered, smaller than line 2,
widely letter-spaced.
Stone: light gray granite, fine crystalline texture,
subtle polish sheen upper left.
Carving: deep V-cuts, upper-left edges bright from raking light,
lower-right edges in shadow.
No background. Stone face fills frame.
Architectural monument quality. Photorealistic.

2. Same granite cornerstone, straight-on view. The incised letters
O A T are filled with polished cast brass inlay, flush with the
stone surface. Brass color warm gold #B8860B, polished, reflecting
raking light from upper left. OWEN'S APPLIED THINKING and EST. MMXXV
remain granite-only incised, no brass fill. The contrast between
gray granite and warm polished brass is the dominant visual.
Photorealistic, architectural monument quality.

3. EST. MMXXV deeply incised, same depth as main letters,
clearly legible, not faint.
