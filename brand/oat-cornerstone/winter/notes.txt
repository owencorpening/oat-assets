Made from oatCornerstoneBuilding.png by scripts/cornerstone-snow.py (numpy/scipy relighting of
the original pixels, like the night variants). Unlike the night variants, the snow caps are
painted in: thin bands that follow the stone's own edges, measured from the photo's luminance
(groove above the cushion, shelf above the block, the block's base on the plinth). The
lettering and texture stay the photo's own.
Re-run:
    python3 scripts/cornerstone-snow.py brand/oat-cornerstone/oatCornerstoneBuilding.png brand/oat-cornerstone/winter
