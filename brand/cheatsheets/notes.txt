Cheatsheets — Brand Asset Notes
=================================

Your own Markdown and HTML cheatsheets, in VS Code's sidebar.
Endorsed OAT tool: wordmark and cards carry "AN OAT TOOL".

FOLDERS
-------
master/     the three sources everything else is made from
              cheatsheets-hero(.svg, -512.png, -1024.png)       3D hero, transparent; -dark on an ink rounded square
              cheatsheets-icon(.svg, -dark.svg)                 flat plinth tile, 128 grid
              cheatsheets-wordmark(.svg, -dark.svg)             icon + name + AN OAT TOOL
exports/    one file per platform use (see resolution-matrix.md); exports/svg/ holds the vector
            source of each, so cheatsheets-export.sh can rebuild the PNG/ICO files on its own
motion/     short loops of the hero, each a still (.png) and a seamless loop (.mp4)
icon/ wordmark/ banner/ icon-hero/
            the earlier kit, unchanged; paths already in use keep working

PLATFORMS
---------
VS Code extension (Marketplace publishing is next): the 128 px Marketplace / Open VSX icon and a monochrome view icon for its sidebar.

MOTION
------
motion/cheatsheets-calm.png / .mp4  — On ink: a slow sheen passes over the hero and its brass detail breathes; 6 s loop.
motion/cheatsheets-night-spotlight.png / .mp4  — Night on a stone backdrop: two warm uplights sweep slowly; 4 s loop.
motion/cheatsheets-night-storm.png / .mp4  — Stormy night: rain at three depths, a double lightning strike and a softer one, neon tracing the brass detail in the product’s light accent, one flicker; 6 s loop.
motion/cheatsheets-snow.png / .mp4  — Winter dusk: snow falling at three depths with a slight drift, snow lying on the hero’s top faces and on the ground, the brass detail glowing warm; 6 s loop.
All 1536×1024 (the cornerstone's night size), H.264, yuv420p, 24 fps, no audio, faststart.
Every loop is seamless: rain and snow move a whole number of screen heights per loop, lights
and glows run on whole cycles.
On a web page: <video autoplay muted loop playsinline poster="cheatsheets-calm.png" src="cheatsheets-calm.mp4"></video>

RULES
-----
- Granite is shared with the OAT neutrals; the brass fold is what marks it.
- Clear space around the icon: one lip height (one eighth of the icon).
- Small sizes (favicons, toolbar, 16–48 px) use the flat tile; the 3D hero is for 256 px and up.

HOW IT WAS MADE
---------------
Drawn in code by the brand kit (project files brand/brand-kit/; belongs in oat-tools):
    python3 gen.py    BRANDDIR cheatsheets       icon/, wordmark/, banner/, brand sheet
    python3 heroes.py BRANDDIR cheatsheets       icon-hero/ (3D hero)
    python3 assets.py BRANDDIR BRANDDIR cheatsheets   master/, exports/, notes, matrix, script, licence
    python3 motion.py BRANDDIR BRANDDIR cheatsheets   motion/ (needs numpy, scipy, Pillow, ffmpeg)
Lettering is Source Serif 4 SemiBold and Inter, converted to outlines.
Made 2026-11-27.
